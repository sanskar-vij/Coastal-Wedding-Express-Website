# Artwork

The three background images come from the existing Coastal Wedding asset library prepared in this conversation. They are clean recreated scene backgrounds, not flattened PDF pages. Original asset filenames and local IDs are preserved.

Pages 1: station. Pages 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7: coastal bridge. Pages 6 and 8: garden. These three available asset backgrounds are reused across the eight PDF sections. No new artwork was generated for this stage.

Local fonts and their included licence files, and the existing wedding music, are retained for later stages.

The active bridge scene now uses the exact PNG attached by the user, saved as the user-reference v02 asset. The earlier bridge asset is retained as an unused source version.

The train animation reuses the transparent two-row locomotive/coach sprite made earlier in this conversation from the user train reference. See public/assets/Train-Artwork-Notes.txt. It is a separate asset, not painted into the backgrounds.
